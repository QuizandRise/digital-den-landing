# 7. Conversation, Offer and Contract Integrity

## 7.1 Negotiation flow

> **Required transaction flow**  
> Search → Profile or Team → Secure Conversation → Formal Offer → Acceptance → Funding → Project Workspace → Delivery → Review → Settlement

## 7.2 Formal Offer requirements

- Scope and exclusions.
- Deliverables and file formats.
- Price, currency, platform fee and professional net amount.
- Delivery date and dependencies.
- Milestones and release conditions.
- Number and meaning of revisions.
- Acceptance criteria and review window.
- Cancellation, refund and partial-completion rules.
- Intellectual-property transfer or licence terms.
- Accessibility or format requirements where relevant.

## 7.3 Version control

Any material change to price, scope, delivery date, milestones,
revisions or acceptance criteria must create a new Offer version. The
previous version remains available in the audit history. Work should
proceed only after the affected parties accept the revised version.

# 8. Reviews, Reputation and Appeals

## 8.1 Review model

- Blind two-way reviews until both parties submit or the review period ends.
- Separate dimensions for quality, communication, scope discipline, professionalism and timing.
- Written feedback with a right of public response.
- Outlier control so one rating cannot destroy a long record.
- No review before meaningful delivery or project closure.
- Clear prohibition on review threats, review purchasing and review exchange.

## 8.2 Review challenges

A review may be challenged where it contains threats, discrimination,
off-topic claims, provably false factual statements, retaliation for
refusing out-of-scope work, or a conflict with a formal dispute outcome.
The platform should preserve legitimate negative experience while
removing abusive or manipulative content.

# 9. Disputes, Holds and Human Review

## 9.1 Three-stage model

| **Stage**           | **Who acts**           | **Effect**                                                                                    |
|---------------------|------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Dispute Request     | Client or Professional | Records the concern; no automatic hold.                                                       |
| Triage              | Authorised Manager     | Reviews initial evidence, seeks clarification and decides whether a formal case is justified. |
| Formal Dispute Case | Authorised Manager     | May apply proportionate holds, collect evidence, propose remedies and issue a decision.       |

## 9.2 Hold policy

- No hold on request submission alone.
- Each hold type must be separately justified: payment, completion, timeout or file access.
- Unrelated funds and projects must remain unaffected.
- Hold duration and review date must be visible to affected parties.
- Automatic escalation if a hold exceeds its service-level deadline.
- Release must be immediate when justification ends.

## 9.3 Remedy ladder

1. Clarification or corrected delivery.
2. Agreed extension.
3. Replacement or reassignment.
4. Partial acceptance and proportionate payment.
5. Price reduction or partial refund.
6. Full refund and termination where justified.
7. Account action only where conduct or safety evidence independently supports it.
